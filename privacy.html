Privacy Policy

Opportunity Trend Research is a local-first research tool that compares Pinterest trend information with Google Trends data while keeping each source clearly identified.

Effective September 21, 2026
This project is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pinterest or Google. Pinterest and Google are trademarks of their respective owners.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Opportunity Trend Research project website and its locally operated research application. The application is intended for the project owner and authorized internal users; it is not currently offered as a public consumer service.

2. Information processed

The application may process the minimum information required to complete a requested trend analysis:

  • keywords, regions, date ranges, and research options submitted by an authorized user;
  • trend information returned by Pinterest and Google, kept separately and labeled by source;
  • Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens when Pinterest API access is enabled; and
  • limited operational records such as request timestamps, completion status, and error diagnostics.

3. Information not collected

The application does not request or store Pinterest passwords, Pinterest browser session cookies, payment information, contact lists, or advertising profiles. It does not use Pinterest data to identify individuals or build behavioral profiles.

4. Purpose of processing

Information is processed only to:

  • perform a trend lookup requested by an authorized user;
  • present Pinterest and Google results side by side without misrepresenting one source as another;
  • cache or retain results when necessary for internal product-opportunity research; and
  • maintain security, reliability, and diagnostic records for the application.

5. Pinterest OAuth

Pinterest access is authorized through Pinterest's OAuth process. OAuth tokens are treated as confidential credentials, stored only in the application's private runtime environment, and never exposed to task callers or included in public source code. Users may revoke access through Pinterest or remove the stored tokens from the application environment.

6. Storage and retention

The application runs in a private environment controlled by the operator. Research inputs, results, caches, and operational logs may remain in that environment until they are no longer needed or are deleted by the operator. OAuth tokens are retained only while the Pinterest connection remains authorized and necessary for the application.

No Pinterest credentials or research records are intentionally stored by this static policy website.

7. Sharing and sale

The project does not sell personal information or Pinterest data. Information is not shared with unrelated third parties. Data is transmitted only to service providers required to fulfill the requested research, including Pinterest and Google, subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and research data, including restricted access, separation of secrets from public files, and omission of tokens and passwords from application logs. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your controls

An authorized user may stop using the application, revoke Pinterest authorization, or request deletion of locally retained research data. Because the application is locally operated, the project owner controls the corresponding application files, databases, caches, and credentials.

10. Children's privacy

The application is an internal research utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a material update is published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue in the Opportunity Trend Research GitHub repository. Do not include access tokens, account credentials, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
